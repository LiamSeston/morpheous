Core structure is 5mm foamboard, scored and folded rather than glued flat, which gives
the walls a slight lean that reads as structural settling once painted. Window and door
frames are laser-cut MDF offcuts from a separate project.

## Paint & Basing

Base coat is a 3:1 mix of grey primer and near-black, dry-brushed up through several
greys before a targeted rust wash on the metal fittings.
