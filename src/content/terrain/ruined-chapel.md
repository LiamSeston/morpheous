## Build Notes

Central ruined chapel piece for the board — two storeys with a collapsed roof section and removable upper floor for interior access during games.

### Current Progress

- Base structure carved from 2" XPS foam
- Window frames cut from plasticard
- Roof timbers from balsa stripwood

### Next Steps

- Add rubble scatter around the base
- Texture walls with cork and sand
- Paint and weather to match the rest of the board
